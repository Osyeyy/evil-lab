The river valley was first settled by farmers who grew barley and kept small herds of goats. Over several centuries the villages grew into market towns, connected by roads that followed the course of the river. Trade in wool and grain brought prosperity, and the towns built stone bridges, granaries and churches that still stand today.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. In the light-dependent reactions, water is split and oxygen is released, while the energy captured is stored in ATP and NADPH. These molecules then power the Calvin cycle, in which carbon dioxide is fixed into sugars.
